# Canonical brand mark

The Canonical mark combines a modular `C` with a plus sign. The four ring
segments represent observable systems and control coverage; the plus connects
the identity to `canonical.plus` without relying on a generic shield, lock, or
cloud.

## Assets

- `public/brand/canonical-mark.svg` — production master, transparent background
- `canonical-mark-concept.png` — image-generation concept used to establish the direction
- `public/favicon.svg` and `public/favicon.ico` — simplified small-size variants
- `public/brand/canonical-cloud.svg` — Canonical Cloud company logo used in the
  site header and footer: three concentric rings, green (`#5ade90`) above and
  cyan (`#10b1c8`) below, mirrored across a central gap, with the inner rings
  ending in horizontal bars. Vector redraw of `canonical-cloud-logo-source.png`
  (ring width 50, gap 17 on the 1254px source grid)
- `canonical-cloud-logo-source.png` — approved raster source for the Canonical
  Cloud logo (2026-11-02), shown on deep navy `#020716`
- `explorations/canonical-cloud-layered-floating.svg` — earlier layered
  Canonical Cloud direction with an asymmetric floating upper terminal
  (superseded by `public/brand/canonical-cloud.svg`)
- `explorations/canonical-cloud-layered-floating-concept.png` — raster concept
  for that earlier direction

## Color

- Emerald: `#34d399`
- Teal: `#2dd4bf`
- Deep navy: `#0a0f1a`

Use the two-color master on dark navy or a one-color emerald/teal treatment
when the surrounding UI supplies the background. Keep clear space around the
mark equal to the thickness of one ring segment. Do not add a shield, checkmark,
cloud, gradient, glow, or drop shadow.
