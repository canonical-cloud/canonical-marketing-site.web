# Readiness-first marketing audit — August 21, 2026

## Decision

Canonical Plus is positioned as readiness and remediation support for software and cloud teams. It does not present itself as an independent auditor, attestation provider, certification body, 3PAO, C3PAO, QSA, regulator, accreditation body, or legal adviser.

## Findings removed from the public site

The previous landing page contained unsupported or prohibited claims about active audit engagements, licensed CPA delivery, Big Four savings, fixed readiness timelines, first-pass success, formal audit execution, FedRAMP authorization delivery, and an attributed customer testimonial. Those claims conflicted with the Canonical Cloud public claims register and were removed rather than softened with a disclaimer.

## Public information architecture

- `/` — readiness-first positioning, outputs, process, featured frameworks, and preliminary assessment CTA.
- `/readiness/` — readiness versus independent assurance, assessment phases, and expected artifacts.
- `/frameworks/` — security, privacy, cloud, payment, healthcare, public-sector, and digital-regulation readiness catalog.
- `/compare/` — conservative comparison of readiness support, compliance automation platforms, independent firms, and internal programs.
- `https://app.canonical.plus/u/quote` — exact authenticated assessment entry point.

## Competitive review

Official public product pages reviewed on August 21, 2026:

- Vanta
- Drata
- Secureframe
- Sprinto
- Thoropass

The category baseline is continuous readiness: integrations, automated evidence, live control status, gap visibility, control reuse, recurring monitoring, and auditor or expert collaboration. Canonical Plus does not claim parity with mature broad-integration platforms. Its current differentiated position is technical scoping, engineering-aware remediation, reusable framework mapping, evidence design, and an explicit independent-review handoff.

## Enforced claims boundary

CI rejects the retired unsupported audit, CPA, guarantee, testimonial, savings, and success-rate language. Comparisons must remain dated, official-source-based, conservative, and explicit about current Canonical Plus limitations.
